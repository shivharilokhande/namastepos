  2. Cross-tenant staff roster dump (FB-17 half-closed). staffPicker takes req.body.businessId with no ownership check — any authed user can enumerate another restaurant's PIN-holding staff. Reject when it ≠ req.user.businessId.backend · src/controllers/authController.js:585
  3. Every offline status update is silently lost. Outbox injects clientId into all queued bodies; the status PUT's Joi rejects unknown keys → 400 → instant dead-letter. Offline cancels keep counting as revenue. Inject clientId only on order-create.flutter · lib/services/offline_outbox.dart:67
  4. Logout never wipes local SQLite or the outbox. On a shared phone the next restaurant inherits the previous tenant's orders/customers, and stale money mutations replay under the new token. Wipe both DBs in logoutFull.flutter · lib/services/auth_service.dart:314
  5. window.__ffGetToken re-exposes the in-memory token. Crisp and Razorpay scripts run on the authed origin and can call the global — undoing the L-1 migration. Gate behind import.meta.env.DEV.dashboard · src/api/client.ts:61

Product read — the sharp-PM take

Your bugs are concentrated exactly in your positioning. NamastePOS pitches three things a legacy POS can't do: real GST compliance, offline-first reliability, and staff-friendly shared-device login. The review found the worst defect in each: the GSTR export a CA needs 401s on every click, offline cancellations vanish silently, and a shared phone leaks one restaurant's data into the next. None of these show up in tests — they show up in the exact moments that decide renewal. The hardening sprint isn't tech debt; it's the product promise.

The riskiest assumption is no longer in the code. 51 features, a 5-tier ladder, live billing — the engineering is ahead of distribution. Every open founder thread (Meta WhatsApp, directory backlinks, GBP verification) is GTM, and none has a measurable hypothesis attached. After P0: pick one acquisition experiment and instrument it. The strongest candidate is WhatsApp-first onboarding once the Meta number connects — OTP-over-WhatsApp is already built, it's the channel Indian restaurant owners actually live in, and it costs nothing to test against the current email/Google flow.

You would not have known any of this was broken. Sentry has never fired in production, the webhook dedup silently no-ops, dead-lettered offline orders vanish without a counter, and a yearly subscriber quietly keeps a free tier. The cheapest product investment this month is a revenue-integrity cron: daily reconciliation of active subscriptions vs. plan rows, dead-letter counts, and refund-pending age — one email to you when anything drifts. It converts "we'd never know" into "we know by breakfast".

What to explicitly not do right now: no new POS features. The mobile-parity list and the nice-to-have admin items (tags/segments, lead pipeline) cost focus that P0 + one growth experiment need. Revisit after the first ten paying tenants renew.
