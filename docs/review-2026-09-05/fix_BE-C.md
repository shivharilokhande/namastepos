Design notes worth knowing:
- Per-line GST allocation is integer paise, weighted by `taxable × slab` (single slab ⇒ plain proportional), remainder on the heaviest line ⇒ Σ lines == `orders.tax` exactly; composition scheme ⇒ 0. Under `ORDER_TAX_ENFORCE=log` with a client-sent tax that differs from the server, the lines still sum to what the bill charged.
- Invoice `discount_paise` now = cashier/settle discount **+ loyalty redemption** (one discount column in the schema). If the components ever fail to re-add to `Σ orders.total` (pre-014 rows, hand edits) the residual goes to `round_off_paise` with a `logger.warn` — the invoice always foots to what was paid.
- #8 lockout state is shared with the manager's login PIN (same row, same person) — five wrong discount PINs also lock that manager's login PIN for 15 min. Deliberate; documented in the code.
- #5: an offline device replaying a KOT against a session that was settled meanwhile now gets 400 `INVALID_SESSION` instead of attaching to a closed bill.
