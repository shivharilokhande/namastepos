NamastePOS is an all-in-one point-of-sale and billing platform for Indian restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens and bars. It covers the full front-of-house and back-of-house workflow: GST-compliant billing and tax invoices, kitchen order tickets and a kitchen display system, QR-code ordering for dine-in and takeaway, table and floor management, menu with variants and modifiers, inventory and dish wastage tracking, split payments, wallets, gift cards and loyalty programs, staff roles with PIN login, and rich sales and P&L reports.
