FAQ

Questions, answered

What is restaurant billing software?
It is the software that takes an order, prints or shares the bill, records the payment and keeps a record you can check at day-end. In NamastePOS it also fires the KOT to the kitchen, handles UPI, cash, card and split payments, and writes every order to the phone first so a network drop cannot stop billing.
How much does restaurant billing software cost?
Starter is free forever at ₹0 for a single counter. Growth is ₹299 a month or ₹2,990 a year. Pro is ₹799 a month or ₹7,990 a year. Advanced is ₹999 a month or ₹9,990 a year. Enterprise is ₹1,999 a month or ₹19,990 a year. Prices are per register, billed monthly with no annual lock-in, and yearly works out to about two months free.
Which plans print GST tax invoices?
GST tax invoices with CGST, SGST or IGST and HSN per item start on the Pro plan at ₹799 a month. E-invoice formats start on Advanced at ₹999 a month. Starter and Growth print plain invoices and receipts, not GST tax invoices.
Do I need a billing machine or a computer?
No. An Android phone is enough to run the counter. Add a Bluetooth or network thermal printer if you want printed bills and KOTs, and use the web dashboard on any laptop for reports. iOS and web are supported too.
