Common questions

Do I need expensive hardware?
No. Any Android phone or tablet with a working internet connection. A Bluetooth or Wi-Fi thermal printer is optional; we have a free print agent for desktop printers too.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
You can export everything as JSON, CSV or PDF from Settings → Privacy & data. Then click "Delete my account" — we anonymise your direct identifiers immediately and retain only what GST law forces us to (tax invoices for 8 years).
Is my data shared with other restaurants?
Never. Every business is fully isolated — separate user accounts, separate menus, separate orders, separate customer database. Cross-tenant access is enforced at the database query level, not as a UI filter.
How do I get paid?
Customers pay you directly through Razorpay (UPI, cards, wallets, netbanking). Funds land in your bank in T+1. We don't touch the money.
Do you support multiple outlets?
Yes — on the Enterprise plan. Each outlet is its own business with its own staff and menu, but you can roll up reports across all of them in one consolidated view.
