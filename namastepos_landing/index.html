When the net goes

The bill still prints. The order still counts.

Most billing software waits for the server and leaves a queue standing. NamastePOS writes the order to the phone first, so the counter never pauses — and every order carries its own key, so replaying it when the network comes back cannot create a second bill or a second charge.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which plans include GST tax invoices?
GST-compliant CGST/SGST tax invoices, with HSN per item, are included from Pro (₹799/mo) and above. E-invoice formats start on Advanced (₹999/mo). Starter and Growth print plain invoices and receipts, not GST tax invoices.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Billing and KOTs keep working with no internet and sync automatically when you're back online — service never stops during a rush.
Can I run it on my phone?
Absolutely. NamastePOS is mobile-first — run the whole restaurant from an Android phone or tablet, no bulky terminal required. iOS and web are supported too.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. Whichever plan you pick starts with a 7-day free trial — no credit card. Pick Pro and you run Pro for 7 days, with GST tax invoices switched on.
What happens after the 7 days?
Nothing is charged. There is no card on file, so no automatic billing can happen. Your account moves to the free Starter plan and you carry on billing, with the paid features switched off until you choose to upgrade.
Can my staff use their own phones?
Yes. Staff sign in with their own phone number and a PIN, on their own Android or iOS phone. Starter includes 1 staff login, Growth 5 and Pro 10; Advanced and Enterprise are unlimited.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. CSV exports of sales, orders and menu data are included from Growth upwards, and there is no lock-in period — stop at the end of any month and take your data with you.
Is there any lock-in contract?
No lock-in. Pricing is all-inclusive with no hidden report paywalls, and you can leave anytime.
How is NamastePOS different from legacy POS systems?
Same everyday features, but mobile-first and offline-ready at a lower, all-inclusive price — no annual lock-in and no paywall to see your own reports.
