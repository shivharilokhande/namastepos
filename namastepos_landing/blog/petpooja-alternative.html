Petpooja Alternative: Why Restaurants Are Switching to NamastePOS

Updated 26 Aug 2026·6 min read·Comparison

Petpooja is a capable, established restaurant POS. But many Indian restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens are looking for something lighter, cheaper and less locked-in. Here's an honest look at what to compare — and where NamastePOS fits.

If you're searching for a Petpooja alternative, you probably already know what you need — you just want it faster, cheaper, or without the parts that annoy you. This guide walks through the criteria that actually matter when you switch a restaurant POS, and how NamastePOS approaches each one.

What to look for in a Petpooja alternative

Switching your point of sale mid-service is risky, so evaluate on the things that affect daily operations, not just the feature checklist:

  • Offline reliability — does billing keep working when the internet drops?
  • Total price — the real monthly cost including add-ons, not just the headline number.
  • Lock-in — annual contracts vs. leave-anytime.
  • Hardware — does it run on a phone, or do you need a bulky terminal?
  • GST compliance — correct CGST/SGST invoices and e-invoice readiness out of the box.
  • Your data — can you see your own reports without a paywall?
