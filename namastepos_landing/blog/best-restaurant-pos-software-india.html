Best Restaurant POS Software in India (2026): How to Choose

Updated 26 Aug 2026·7 min read·Buyer's Guide

There's no single "best" POS — there's the best one for how you serve. This guide lists the ten things that actually matter for Indian restaurants, so you can judge any option (including ours) on merit.

The 10 things that actually matter

1. Offline reliability

Indian internet is not always dependable during a dinner rush. Your POS must keep billing and firing KOTs offline, then sync automatically. This is non-negotiable — a POS that freezes when Wi-Fi drops costs you covers.

2. Correct GST billing

Compliant CGST/SGST invoices, sequential numbering, and e-invoice readiness should be built in, not an add-on. See our GST billing guide for what a compliant bill needs.

3. Fast KOT and kitchen display

Orders must reach the kitchen instantly, with a live display (KDS) and clear ticketing. Seconds per order add up across a busy service.

4. Runs on a phone

Mobile-first means no expensive terminal, easy captain ordering, and staff who already know the device. Look for real Android/tablet support, not just a shrunk-down desktop app.

5. QR self-ordering

Let guests scan and order from the table. It reduces wait times and frees staff, and it's increasingly expected by younger diners.

6. Every payment method

Cash, UPI, card, and split payments — settle a bill however the table wants to pay, without workarounds.

7. Loyalty and repeat business

Points, memberships and a wallet turn one-time diners into regulars. Cheaper than winning a new customer every time.

8. Reports you can actually see

Live sales, item performance, P&L and GST — and crucially, without a paywall on your own data. If seeing your numbers costs extra, that's a red flag.

9. Transparent, all-inclusive pricing

Beware headline prices with essential features unbundled. Add up the real monthly cost including the add-ons you'll actually need.

10. No lock-in

You should be able to leave anytime. Annual contracts make sense for the vendor, not always for you.

Quick checklist before you buy

  • Does billing work offline and sync later?
  • Are GST invoices compliant and e-invoice ready?
  • Can it run on the phones you already own?
  • Is QR ordering and KDS included?
  • Are reports free to view?
  • Is the price all-inclusive with no lock-in?
  • Can you set it up in an afternoon?
