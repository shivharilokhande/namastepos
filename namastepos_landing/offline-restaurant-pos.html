Offline billing software: a restaurant POS that works without internet

The broadband drops at 8:40pm with fourteen tables seated. Here is exactly what NamastePOS does next.

What happens when the network goes:

  1. The order is written to the phone the moment you take it. The device is the record, not a browser tab.
  2. The KOT still fires to your kitchen printer or the kitchen display. The bill still prints. Cash and UPI are still recorded.
  3. When the connection returns, the held orders replay in order, each with the ID it was created with.
  4. The server accepts each ID once. A retry cannot create a second bill, so nobody is charged twice.

There is no offline mode to switch on and nothing to remember mid-rush. Offline billing is part of the core POS, so it is on every plan including the free one.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does the billing keep working without internet?
Yes. NamastePOS writes each order to the phone as it is taken, so the counter does not depend on the network to bill. You keep taking orders, firing KOTs, printing bills and recording cash or UPI. There is no offline mode to switch on and nothing to remember during a rush.
What happens to my bills while the phone is offline?
They are held on the device with their invoice numbers and totals intact. When the connection comes back the app replays them in order, and they appear in your reports and in the web dashboard exactly as they were billed. Nothing is retyped and nothing is lost.
Can a bill get charged twice when it syncs?
No. Every order carries an ID generated on the device, and the server accepts that ID only once. If the phone retries because a reply went missing, the second attempt is recognised and ignored rather than creating a duplicate bill. This is the difference between a real offline POS and a queue that hopes for the best.
Is offline billing on the free plan?
Yes. Offline billing is part of the core POS, so it is on every plan including Starter at ₹0. Paid plans start at ₹299 a month per register and add loyalty, WhatsApp, reports and higher limits. GST tax invoices start on Pro at ₹799 a month.

Test it the honest way

Install the app, add two items, put the phone in airplane mode and take an order. Then turn the network back on and watch it land once.
